## Title

Drum Bun road rules

## Purpose (description)

Help drivers of private cars, campers and motorcycles who travel between Romania and Germany or Austria through Hungary. They need to know which vignettes and tolls to buy for their dates, what each costs, where to buy it, which winter-tyre, emission-zone and border rules apply, and which claims they read online are out of date. Official sources (the issuer, a ministry or the law) outrank automobile clubs, press, blogs and forums; between official sources the newer one wins, and the law wins over a web page.
