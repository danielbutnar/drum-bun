# Austria: what a private driver needs (car ≤ 3.5 t, camper, trailer, motorcycle)

Research date: 2026-09-29. Scope: a Romanian-registered private vehicle crossing Austria between Hungary (Hegyeshalom/Nickelsdorf) and Germany (Walserberg, Suben, Kufstein), or driving to Vienna, Linz, Salzburg or Graz. Official sources first (asfinag.at, the ASFINAG toll regulations "Mautordnung", RIS legal texts, oesterreich.gv.at, bmimi.gv.at). ÖAMTC, ADAC, EVZ and news sites are marked as secondary.

## Facts

### Vignette: who needs it, products, prices

- Every motorway and expressway (Autobahn, Schnellstraße) needs a vignette for "Pkw, Motorrädern und Wohnmobilen bis 3,5 t tzGm" (tzGm = technically permissible laden mass). [https://www.asfinag.at/maut-vignette/vignette/, DE, no date shown, prices marked valid from 1 Dec 2025]
- Two vignette categories: A = single-track vehicles (motorcycles; three-wheelers count as single-track, § 10(3) BStMG), B = multi-track vehicles up to 3.5 t tzGm (cars, campers). [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 1.1–1.2, EN, valid from 1 Jan 2026]
- Products: annual, 2-month, 10-day and 1-day vignette. The 1-day vignette is "digitally only". Sticker and digital vignettes cost the same. [Folder 2026, EN, Oct 2025]
- 2026 prices (incl. 20% VAT), set by the Vignettenpreisverordnung 2025 (BGBl II Nr. 225/2025, "Ausgegeben am 22. Oktober 2025"), for vignettes valid from 1 Dec 2025: motorcycle annual €42.70, 2-month €12.80, 10-day €5.10, 1-day €3.80; car annual €106.80, 2-month €32.00, 10-day €12.80, 1-day €9.60. [https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/BgblAuth/BGBLA_2025_II_225/BGBLA_2025_II_225.html, DE, 22 Oct 2025] [Folder 2026, EN, Oct 2025] [Mautordnung v87 Anhang 1: https://media.asfinag.at/media/5tbhzhd1/01_mo_v87_anhang-1-vignette.pdf, DE, valid from 1 Jan 2026]
- 2025 prices, set by the Vignettenpreisverordnung 2024 (BGBl II Nr. 307/2024, "Ausgegeben am 11. November 2024"), for vignettes valid from 1 Dec 2024: motorcycle annual €41.50, 2-month €12.40, 10-day €4.90, 1-day €3.70; car annual €103.80, 2-month €31.10, 10-day €12.40, 1-day €9.30. [https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/BgblAuth/BGBLA_2024_II_307/BGBLA_2024_II_307.html, DE, 11 Nov 2024] [Folder 2025, DE, Oct 2024]
- The 2026 prices were raised by 2.9% in line with the harmonised consumer price index: "Die Vignettentarife [werden] jährlich an den harmonisierten Verbraucherpreisindex (HVPI) angepasst und für 2026 um 2,9 Prozent angehoben". [https://www.asfinag.at/ueber-uns/presse/pressemeldungen/klebevignette-2026-wird-feuerrot/, DE, 8 Oct 2025]
- Legal basis for the yearly price change: § 12(5) BStMG. The minister adjusts prices every year by the change in the annual average HVPI (previous year vs. the year before), rounded to 10 cents. New prices apply to the next year's annual vignette and to short-term vignettes valid from 1 December of the year the ordinance is issued. [https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20002090&Paragraf=12, DE, consolidated version checked 29 Sep 2026]
- When prices appear (for "which price is current" logic): 2024 prices announced 29 Sep 2023 [https://www.asfinag.at/ueber-uns/presse/pressemeldungen/neue-klebevignette-2024-traegt-die-farbe-sonnengelb/, DE, 29 Sep 2023]; 2025 prices by ordinance on 11 Nov 2024 (see above); 2026 prices announced 8 Oct 2025 and set by ordinance on 22 Oct 2025 (see above). So new prices have appeared between late September and mid-November; the switch date is always 1 December. No official 2027 price was found on 29 Sep 2026 (see Unverified).
- Sale start of the next year's products: digital vignette 2025 on sale from 12 Nov 2024, sticker from 21 Nov 2024 [https://www.asfinag.at/ueber-uns/presse/pressemeldungen/verkaufsstart-klebevignette-2025/, DE, 21 Nov 2024]; digital vignette 2026 from 4 Nov 2025, sticker from 20 Nov 2025 [https://www.asfinag.at/ueber-uns/presse/pressemeldungen/klebevignette-ab-sofort-erhaeltlich/, DE, 20 Nov 2025].
- Official sellers: ASFINAG toll shop https://shop.asfinag.at (EN: https://shop.asfinag.at/en), the free ASFINAG app, ASFINAG toll stations, ASFINAG vending machines (cashless), and around 6,000 points of sale (petrol stations, tobacconists, ÖAMTC, ARBÖ, ADAC offices). List of sales outlets: https://www.asfinag.at/vertriebsstellen. [Folder 2026, EN, Oct 2025] [https://www.asfinag.at/maut-vignette/vignette/digitale-vignette/, DE, no date]
- Public check of any plate: the vignette registry https://evidenz.asfinag.at/ shows free of charge for which period a digital vignette or digital section toll is registered. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 1.1, EN, valid from 1 Jan 2026]

### Vignette: validity rules

- Annual vignette: valid for the year printed on it and usable "from 1 December of the previous year to 31 January of the following year" (14 months). The 2026 annual vignette runs from 1 Dec 2025 to 31 Jan 2027 inclusive. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 1.5.1] [Folder 2026, EN, Oct 2025]
- 2-month vignette: runs two months from the first day of validity and "expires at the end of the day of the second month that corresponds to the date on which [it] came into effect", or at the end of the last day of that month if no such date exists. ASFINAG example: "valid from January 10th, validity expires March 10th, midnight." [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 1.5.2] [https://www.asfinag.at/en/toll/vignette/faq-vignette/, EN, no date]
- 10-day vignette: "ten consecutive calendar days from the first day of validity". Examples: bought 15 Jan at 07:22 with immediate start, valid until 24 Jan 23:59:59; start chosen as 20 Jan, valid 20 Jan 00:00 to 29 Jan 23:59:59. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 1.5.3 and 3.2.1.1.2] The law says the same: "zehn aufeinanderfolgender Kalendertage". [https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20002090&Paragraf=11, DE, checked 29 Sep 2026]
- 1-day vignette: digital only, valid for one freely chosen calendar day. Bought for today: valid from the time of purchase to 23:59; bought for a later date: 00:00 to 23:59 of that date. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 1.5.4 and 3.2.1.1.3] [https://shop.asfinag.at/en/products/digital-vignette/1-day-vignette/, EN, no date]
- Year boundary: the last possible first day of validity for a 2-month, 10-day or 1-day vignette of a given vignette year is 30 November. A 2026 10-day vignette starting on 30 Nov 2026 runs until 9 Dec 2026; a 2026 2-month vignette starting on 30 Nov 2026 runs until 30 Jan 2027. Any start date in December needs a product of the following year (so from 1 Dec 2026, 2027 products and 2027 prices). [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 1.5.2–1.5.4]
- Sticker vignettes for 2 months and 10 days are punched at the point of sale; "If these toll stickers are not punched, they are invalid." A sticker that is only carried in the car (glove box, parking-ticket holder) is not valid. [Folder 2026, EN, Oct 2025]
- Where to stick it: car on the inside of the windscreen (recommended top left or behind the rear-view mirror), not in the tint strip; motorcycle on a clean, non-removable part such as the fork; "A vignette attached to a luggage case or a motorcycle bag is not valid." [Folder 2026, EN, Oct 2025]

### Vignette: the 18-day rule and how to get immediate validity

- Rule (ASFINAG leaflet): "Digital vignette (annual, 2-month vignette): As a private customer, you can withdraw from the online purchase of the digital vignette within 14 days. Taking into account a further three-day period for mail delivery, your digital vignette is valid from the 18th day after purchase. These restrictions do not apply to business customers. Digital vignette (10-day, 1-day vignette): Valid immediately. Digital section toll: Valid immediately." [Folder 2026, EN, Oct 2025]
- Same rule in German: "2-Monats- und Jahres-Vignetten sind aufgrund des Konsumentenschutzes auch weiterhin frühestens ab dem 18. Tag nach dem Online-Kauf gültig." [https://www.asfinag.at/maut-vignette/vignette/, DE, no date] "Beim Kauf einer 1-Tages bzw. 10-Tages Vignette entfällt diese Frist!" [https://www.asfinag.at/maut-vignette/vignette/digitale-vignette/, DE, no date]
- The app counts as online: "If the digital vignette is purchased in the ASFINAG toll shop or via the ASFINAG app, it is only valid from the 18th day after the online purchase." followed by "This deadline does not apply if you purchase a 1-day or 10-day vignette!" [https://www.asfinag.at/en/toll/vignette/faq-vignette/, EN, no date]
- How to count: first valid day = purchase date + 18 days. Mautordnung example: bought 15 Jan, first day of validity 2 Feb at the earliest. The ASFINAG shop, read on 29 Sep 2026, said a 2-month or annual vignette bought that day is valid "from 17.10.2026 at the earliest". [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 3.2.1.1.1] [https://shop.asfinag.at/en/products/digital-vignette/1-day-vignette/, EN, read 29 Sep 2026]
- Immediate validity for annual and 2-month vignettes: buy at a point of sale, an ASFINAG toll station or an ASFINAG vending machine. "Wenn Sie über einen unserer Vertriebspartner, an der Mautstelle oder einem Automaten [kaufen] ... entfällt die 18-Tage Frist: sie gilt dann sofort". [https://www.asfinag.at/maut-vignette/vignette/digitale-vignette/, DE, no date] The Mautordnung: an annual vignette bought at a vending machine, toll station or point of sale "is valid immediately"; for 2-month, 10-day and 1-day vignettes the first day can be chosen freely there. There is no right of withdrawal for these purchases. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 3.2.2.1, 3.2.3.1, 3.3]
- 10-day and 1-day bought online: immediate validity requires the buyer to "expressly request" early fulfilment of the contract. For the 1-day vignette the withdrawal right then ends when validity starts; for the 10-day a pro-rata fee applies on withdrawal. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 3.2.1.1.2, 3.2.1.1.3, 3.3]
- Businesses buying for their business confirm this in the shop, have no withdrawal right, and can choose the purchase day as first day of validity. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 3.4]
- The 18-day rule is not in the toll act (BStMG § 11 has no such text); ASFINAG ties it to the consumer 14-day withdrawal right for online purchases. [https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20002090&Paragraf=11, DE, checked 29 Sep 2026] [Folder 2026, EN, Oct 2025]

### Vignette: end of the sticker

- 2026 is the last sticker year (colour "feuerrot"). "ab dem 1. Februar 2027 gibt es ausschließlich digitale Varianten in Österreich". [https://www.asfinag.at/ueber-uns/presse/pressemeldungen/klebevignette-2026-wird-feuerrot/, DE, 8 Oct 2025]
- In law: for 1-day, 10-day, 2-month and annual vignettes "die ab dem 1. Dezember 2026 zur Benützung der Mautstrecken berechtigen", the time-based toll is paid only by registering the plate (digital vignette). [§ 11 BStMG, https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20002090&Paragraf=11, DE, checked 29 Sep 2026]
- Practical result: any vignette whose validity starts on or after 1 Dec 2026 is digital only; a 2026 sticker bought earlier stays valid until its own end date (annual: 31 Jan 2027). [same sources] Government decision of 8 Oct 2025, with ASFINAG's figure that over 75% of annual and over 50% of short-term vignettes were already digital. [https://www.bmimi.gv.at/service/presse/hanke/2025/1008_vignette.html, DE, 8 Oct 2025]

### Vignette: campers, trailers, motorcycles

- Trailers: "Für Anhänger, die von mehrspurigen Kraftfahrzeugen mit einer technisch zulässigen Gesamtmasse von nicht mehr als 3,5 t gezogen werden, sowie für Beiwagen einspuriger Kraftfahrzeuge ist keine zeitabhängige Maut zu entrichten." One vignette for the towing vehicle only; the trailer's mass does not count. [Mautordnung v87 DE, Teil A I 1.2, valid from 1 Jan 2026] FAQ: "No additional toll sticker is required for trailers, caravans, motorbike sidecars etc." [https://www.asfinag.at/en/toll/vignette/faq-vignette/, EN, no date]
- Same for section tolls: no section toll for trailers towed by vehicles ≤ 3.5 t tzGm or for sidecars. [Mautordnung v87 DE, Teil A II 1.2]
- Campers (Wohnmobile): need the category B vignette if tzGm ≤ 3.5 t. [https://www.asfinag.at/maut-vignette/vignette/, DE, no date]
- The threshold is the technically permissible laden mass, not a downrated registered mass. Transitional rule (§ 33(18) Z 8 BStMG): vehicles first registered before 1 Dec 2023 whose permissible mass was set at ≤ 3.5 t before 1 Dec 2023 count as ≤ 3.5 t "until 31 January 2029". Proof goes to tzgm@asfinag.at. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 1.2 and Part A II 1.2]
- Secondary: the tzGm is field F.1 of the registration certificate; motorhomes with tzGm above 3.5 t need a GO-Box (distance-based toll) instead of a vignette, apart from the transitional rule. [https://www.adac.de/news/maut-oesterreich-wohnmobile/, DE, no date shown, secondary]

### Where no vignette is needed

- Vignette-free motorway sections for vehicles ≤ 3.5 t (§ 13(1a) with § 33(15) BStMG):
  - A 1 Westautobahn between the German border at Walserberg and the Salzburg Nord junction
  - A 12 Inntalautobahn between the German border at Kufstein and the Kufstein-Süd junction
  - A 14 Rheintal/Walgau Autobahn between the German border at Hörbranz and the Hohenems junction
  - A 26 Linzer Autobahn (under construction) needs a vignette only from the end of the day its link to the A 7 via the Linz-Hummelhof junction opens to traffic.
  [Mautordnung v87 DE, Teil A I, list of exemptions, valid from 1 Jan 2026] [https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/en/themen/mobilitaet/kfz/10/Vignette-und-Maut/streckenbezogene_Ausnahmen-von-der-Vignettenpflicht, EN, updated 01.01.2026]
- These exemptions took effect on 15 Dec 2019 through an amendment to the toll act. [https://www.parlament.gv.at/aktuelles/news/archiv/j2019/210mautbefreiung, DE, 2019]
- No such exemption exists at the Hungarian border (A 4 Nickelsdorf) or at Suben (A 8). ASFINAG lists toll-enforcement points at A 4 Nickelsdorf, A 4 Gols, A 4 Zurndorf, A 4 Bruck/Leitha, A 1 Haag, A 1 Wolfsbach, A 1 Eberstalzell, A 1 Walserberg, A 8 Suben and A 8 Kematen, among others, and also checks at border crossings and junctions. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 1.8.1] [Folder 2026 map, EN, Oct 2025]
- Section-toll roads (A 9 Bosruck and Gleinalm tunnels, A 10 Tauern/Katschberg, A 11 Karawanken, A 13 Brenner, S 16 Arlberg): "Auf den Strecken zwischen den einzelnen Mautstellen entfällt die sonst auf Autobahnen und Schnellstraßen übliche Vignettenpflicht." [https://www.asfinag.at/maut-vignette/streckenmaut/, DE, no date] The roads leading to them still need a vignette, and FLEX registration "does NOT replace the purchase of a (digital) vignette!" [https://shop.asfinag.at/en/toll-products/digital-sectiontoll/, EN, no date]

### Section tolls (Streckenmaut)

- Tariffs valid from 1 Jan 2026, one tariff for all vehicles ≤ 3.5 t tzGm (the Mautordnung lists no separate motorcycle rate; Part A II covers both single-track and multi-track vehicles ≤ 3.5 t): A 9 Gleinalm single trip €12.00; A 9 Bosruck €7.00; A 9 multi-trip card €80.00; A 10 Tauern/Katschberg whole section €15.00, subsections €7.50, multi-trip €90.00; A 11 Karawanken single trip €9.00, 14-trip monthly card €40.40; A 13 Brenner whole section €12.50, subsections €1.00 / €3.50 / €4.00 / €6.00, multi-trip €75.00; S 16 Arlberg tunnel €13.00, multi-trip €78.00. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A II 1.2 and 3.1] [https://www.asfinag.at/maut-vignette/streckenmaut/, DE, "Die Tarife sind ab 1.1.2026 gültig."]
- 2025 → 2026 changes: single trips A 9 Gleinalm €11.50 → €12.00, A 9 Bosruck €7.00 → €7.00, A 10 €14.50 → €15.00, A 13 €12.00 → €12.50, S 16 €12.50 → €13.00, A 11 €8.80 → €9.00; multi-trip cards A 9 €77.50 → €80.00, A 10 €87.00 → €90.00, A 13 €72.00 → €75.00, S 16 €75.00 → €78.00. [https://www.ots.at/presseaussendung/OTS_20251230_OTS0007/anpassung-einiger-tarife-fuer-fahrzeuge-bis-35-tonnen-gesamtgewicht-auf-den-streckenmaut-abschnitten-ab-1-jaenner-2026, DE, 30 Dec 2025] [https://media.asfinag.at/media/0semzhqh/aenderungen-mautordnung_v87.pdf, DE, info sheet for v87]
- A single-trip ticket is valid for one year from issue, for one direction. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A II 3.2.1] Digital section toll is valid immediately (no 18-day wait). [Folder 2026, EN, Oct 2025]
- The digital A 9 product combines both tunnels: €19.00 for one trip (Bosruck €7.00 + Gleinalm €12.00). [https://shop.asfinag.at/en/toll-products/digital-sectiontoll/, EN, no date]
- A 11 Karawanken: ASFINAG sells only southbound tickets; the northbound direction is tolled by the Slovenian operator DARS. [https://shop.asfinag.at/en/toll-products/digital-sectiontoll/, EN, no date]
- Where the tolls sit: A 9 Spital am Pyhrn–Ardning (Bosruck tunnel) and St. Michael–Übelbach (Gleinalm tunnel); A 10 Flachau–Rennweg am Katschberg; A 11 St. Jakob im Rosental–Slovenian border; A 13 Brenner; S 16 St. Anton–Langen am Arlberg. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A II 2.1.1]
- Pay at the toll booth (cash, credit, fuel or debit card), buy the digital section toll in the shop, app, vending machines or at partners, or register for "Digital Section Toll FLEX" (billed afterwards; capped at the multi-trip card price per section for 365 days from the first trip). [Folder 2026, EN, Oct 2025]

### Routes Romania ↔ Germany: what is due in Austria

- None of the section-toll segments lies on the A 4, A 1, A 2, A 21, A 23, A 25 or A 8 (see the segment list above and the Folder 2026 map). On these routes the vignette is the only toll for vehicles ≤ 3.5 t. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A II 2.1.1] [Folder 2026 map, EN, Oct 2025]
  - Nickelsdorf (A 4) → Vienna → Linz → Salzburg (A 1) → Walserberg: vignette from the Hungarian border; the last stretch Salzburg Nord → Walserberg is vignette-free. Nothing else is due.
  - Vienna → Graz (A 2): vignette only. The A 9 Gleinalm toll applies only if you drive via the Pyhrn motorway (for example Linz → Graz).
  - Vienna → Linz → A 25 → A 8 → Suben/Passau: vignette only; no free section at Suben.
  - Munich → Salzburg city via Walserberg, if you stay on the A 1 no further than Salzburg Nord: no vignette.
- Other things on these routes: the IG-L 100 km/h zone on the A 1 near Linz and the 100 km/h limit on the A 2 at Graz (see "Speed limits" below).

### Penalties: substitute toll (Ersatzmaut) and fines

- Substitute toll for vignette offences committed from 1 Jan 2026: category A (motorcycles) €100.00, category B (cars and vehicles ≤ 3.5 t) €200.00. Before 1 Jan 2026 it was €65.00 and €120.00. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 1.9.3.2] [https://media.asfinag.at/media/0semzhqh/aenderungen-mautordnung_v87.pdf, DE, info sheet v87] The same €100 / €200 apply to unpaid section tolls. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A II 7.3.2]
- ASFINAG web page: cars and camper vans "200 euros", motorcycles "100 euros"; "Non-payment of the substitute payment incurs heavy administrative fine of between 300 and 3,000 euros." [https://www.asfinag.at/en/toll/service-and-controls/, EN, no date]
- The fine: driving on the toll network without a valid vignette is an administrative offence (§ 20(1) BStMG), punished by the district authority with €300–€3,000. No fine if the substitute toll is paid; a paid substitute toll cannot be reclaimed. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 1.9.1–1.9.2]
- How payment works: on the spot to enforcement officers (cash or listed payment methods); a request left on the car must be paid within two weeks; after automatic camera detection the keeper receives a written request, or for foreign vehicles an "information letter" under § 30b BStMG, payable within four weeks with the exact reference number. Instalments are not accepted (€15 processing fee). [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 1.9.3.1]
- If a foreign driver does not pay and prosecution looks impossible or much harder, officers can take a provisional security deposit and stop the journey (for example by removing keys or documents) under §§ 27–28 BStMG, also for EU-registered vehicles. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 1.8]
- A paid substitute toll covers the vignette network for that day and the following calendar day. Put the receipt behind the windscreen. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 1.9.3.3]
- Repeat cap from 1 Jan 2026: within 30 days, at most two substitute-toll demands, counting vignette and section-toll offences together; further cases are not charged or reported. [https://media.asfinag.at/media/0semzhqh/aenderungen-mautordnung_v87.pdf, DE, info sheet v87]

### Wrong plate on a digital vignette

- A digital vignette is valid only if it is "properly registered in the toll system" for the vehicle's plate. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 1.1] ASFINAG's position on typos (quoted by ORF): "Ist zum Zeitpunkt der Kontrolle kein gültiges Mautprodukt für das korrekte Kennzeichen registriert, müsse die Ersatzmaut jedenfalls bezahlt werden." So a wrong plate is treated like no vignette (now €200 for a car). [https://help.orf.at/stories/3225148/, DE, 25 May 2024, secondary; the amount in the article is the old €120]
- Before validity starts: plate and country of registration can be changed free of charge for all products, in the shop account, at the ASFINAG Service Center, toll stations or the selling point. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 3.5] [https://shop.asfinag.at/en/info-pages/license-plate/, EN, no date]
- After validity starts: "only digital annual vignettes can be re-registered" (1-day, 10-day and 2-month cannot). Fee €18.00. A typo correction is possible but "the new license plate number must not deviate to a large extent from the original entry", and the €18 is not refunded. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 3.6] [https://shop.asfinag.at/en/info-pages/license-plate/, EN, no date] The law caps the fee for a re-registration without reason at €20 incl. VAT. [§ 11 BStMG, RIS, checked 29 Sep 2026]
- At a point of sale the printed order confirmation shows the registered plate; "Any need for correction must be reported immediately at the point of sale." [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 3.2.3]
- A re-registration made in the shop is provisional for ten calendar days while ASFINAG checks the documents; if rejected, the vignette reverts to the old plate. [Mautordnung v87 EN, Part A I 3.6.3]

### Winter equipment

- Cars (M1), light goods vehicles (N1) and closed quadricycles: from 1 November to 15 April, "bei winterlichen Fahrbahnverhältnissen wie insbesondere Schneefahrbahn, Schneematsch oder Eis" the vehicle may be driven only with winter tyres on all wheels, or with snow chains on at least two drive wheels "wenn die Fahrbahn mit einer zusammenhängenden oder nicht nennenswert unterbrochenen Schnee- oder Eisschicht bedeckt ist". [§ 102(8a) KFG, https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10011384&Paragraf=102, DE, checked 29 Sep 2026]
- The rule is situational: it applies only in wintry road conditions, not on a fixed date. [https://www.bmimi.gv.at/en/topics/transport/roads/safety/tyres.html, EN, no date]
- A winter tyre must be marked "M+S", "M.S." or "M&S", or carry a snowflake symbol (in addition or alone); all-weather tyres count only with these markings. [https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/en/themen/mobilitaet/kfz/10/2/Seite.063100, EN, updated 12.01.2026]
- Minimum tread depth for winter tyres on vehicles ≤ 3.5 t: 4 mm (radial), 5 mm (cross-ply). Summer tyres: 1.6 mm. Legal basis § 4 KDV. [https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/en/themen/mobilitaet/kfz/10/Seite.063320, EN, updated 12.01.2026] [https://www.bmimi.gv.at/verkehrssicherheit/massnahmen/winterreifen.html, DE, no date]
- Foreign-registered vehicles are covered: the rule applies to "vehicles registered in Austria as well as those registered abroad". [https://www.evz.de/en/travelling-motor-vehicles/motor-vehicles/winter-tires-within-europe.html, EN, updated 25 Sep 2026, secondary (European Consumer Centre Germany)]
- Motorcycles: § 102(8a) names only M1, N1 and closed quadricycles, so motorcycles are not covered. ÖAMTC confirms motorcycles are exempt. [§ 102(8a) KFG, RIS] [https://www.oeamtc.at/thema/vorschriften-strafen/winterreifenpflicht-in-oesterreich-74581857, DE, no date, secondary]
- Fines differ by source. BMIMI: "eine Geldstrafe von 35 Euro", and "Sollten andere Verkehrsteilnehmerinnen und -teilnehmer gefährdet werden, drohen bis zu 5.000 Euro Strafe." [https://www.bmimi.gv.at/verkehrssicherheit/massnahmen/winterreifen.html, DE, no date] ÖAMTC: "eine Strafe von rund 100 Euro"; if others are endangered "theoretisch sogar bis zu 10.000 Euro". [ÖAMTC, as above, secondary] The law: § 134(1) KFG allows fines "bis zu 10 000 Euro"; that ceiling already applied in the version in force from 20 Jul 2024. [https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10011384&Paragraf=134, DE, checked 29 Sep 2026] (Exact on-the-spot amount: see Unverified.)

### Emergency lane, equipment, environmental zones

- Emergency lane (Rettungsgasse) on motorways and expressways: form it "as soon as the traffic ... is beginning to come to a halt, whether an emergency vehicle is nearby or not". Leftmost lane moves left, all other lanes move right. [https://www.asfinag.at/en/traffic-road-safety/traffic-management/the-emergency-corridor/, EN, no date]
- Fines: "up to 726 euros" for not forming it or using it unlawfully; "between 72 and 2,180 Euro" if this stops emergency vehicles getting through. Legal basis § 46 StVO. [https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/en/themen/mobilitaet/kfz/10/Seite.063130/Seite.065000, EN, updated 12.01.2026]
- Mandatory equipment (§ 102(10) KFG): every driver must carry a first-aid kit ("Verbandzeug ... in einem widerstandsfähigen Behälter staubdicht verpackt"); drivers of multi-track vehicles must also carry a warning triangle and a high-visibility garment to ÖNORM EN 471 or EN ISO 20471 with white retroreflective stripes. So a motorcyclist needs a first-aid kit but no triangle or vest. [§ 102(10) KFG, https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10011384&Paragraf=102, DE, checked 29 Sep 2026]
- When to wear the vest: when setting up the warning triangle (§ 89(2) StVO), and whenever the driver is outside the vehicle on a motorway or expressway after a breakdown (§ 46(3) StVO). [§ 102(10) KFG, RIS] ÖAMTC notes the duty applies to the driver only. [https://www.oeamtc.at/thema/vorschriften-strafen/kfz-mitfuehrpflichten-in-oesterreich-16455483, DE, no date, secondary]
- Environmental zones: "You don't need an environmental badge for your car in Austria." Austrian environmental zones only affect goods vehicles N1–N3, including cars registered as N1. [https://www.austria.info/en-gb/planning/toll-vignette-and-go-box/, EN, no date, Austrian National Tourist Office]

### Speed limits: IG-L air-quality zones and others

- Upper Austria, A 1 near Linz (on the Vienna–Salzburg route): a 100 km/h limit that switches on with nitrogen-dioxide levels, shown on overhead signs with the "IG-L" plate. It covers roughly km 155–168 (Enns-Steyr to Haid area, both directions). [https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=LROO&Gesetzesnummer=20000549, DE, Upper Austrian ordinance, version checked 29 Sep 2026]
- Styria: the IG-L 100 km/h limits on parts of the A 2 and A 9 around Graz ended "mit Wirkung vom 18. April 2025". [https://www.news.steiermark.at/cms/beitrag/12980555/154271055/, DE, 14 Apr 2025] The remaining IG-L 100 on the A 2 between Graz-Ost and Graz-West (Feldkirchen) was repealed with effect from the end of 25 May 2026. From 26 May 2026 an ordinary road-traffic (StVO) limit of 100 km/h applies there, and the IG-L exemption for electric cars no longer applies. [https://www.kommunikation.steiermark.at/cms/beitrag/13015925/374565, DE, 20 May 2026]
- Salzburg: the IG-L 100 on the A 10 between Salzburg and Golling was due to be lifted at the end of October or start of November 2023; the actual date was not checked. [https://salzburg.orf.at/stories/3222469/, DE, 1 Sep 2023, secondary]
- Tyrol (not on the RO–DE routes above): a permanent IG-L 100 km/h on sections of the A 12 (Kufstein/Ebbs–Zirl, Imst–Zams) and the A 13, in force since 20 Nov 2014. [https://www.tirol.gv.at/umwelt/umweltrecht/luftreinhalterecht/100-kmh-luft/, DE, no date]
- IG-L exemption for electric cars: only for battery-electric or hydrogen vehicles with a "Kennzeichentafel mit grüner Schrift gemäß § 49 Absatz 4 Z 5 Kraftfahrgesetz 1967" (Austrian green-lettered plate), and only where signs show the exemption. [https://www.bmimi.gv.at/themen/mobilitaet/alternative_verkehrskonzepte/elektromobilitaet/recht/ig_luft.html, DE, no date]
- IG-L speed fines: up to €2,180 (§ 30 IG-L); on-the-spot fines up to €90 for exceeding by no more than 30 km/h. [https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10011027&Paragraf=30, DE, checked 29 Sep 2026]
- General limits (secondary): cars and motorcycles 130 km/h on motorways; car with a light trailer 100 km/h; car with a heavy trailer 100 km/h (licence class B enough) or 80 km/h (BE or code 96 needed). [https://www.oeamtc.at/thema/vorschriften-strafen/zulaessige-hoechstgeschwindigkeiten-in-oesterreich-16182124, DE, no date, secondary]
