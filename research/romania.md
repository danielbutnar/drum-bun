# Romania: what a private driver needs (car ≤ 3.5 t, camper, trailer, motorcycle)

Research date: 2026-09-29. Scope: route Romania ↔ Hungary ↔ Austria/Germany (A1 Sibiu–Deva–Arad–Nădlac, Borș), plus A2 from Constanța.

**Change on 1 Oct 2026, 00:00.** Rovinieta prices, the official seller, the fines and the Danube bridge tolls all change that day. An answer has to pick the rule by the travel date: up to 30 Sep 2026 23:59 the old system applies, from 1 Oct 2026 the new one.

## Facts

### Rovinietă: legal basis, seller, switch-over

- The legal basis from 1 Oct 2026 is Legea 226/2023 (road tariffs on the national road network). It repeals OG 15/2002. Its start was postponed from 1 Jan 2026 to 1 Jul 2026 by OG 14/2025, then to 1 Oct 2026 by Legea 103/2026. [https://legislatie.just.ro/public/DetaliiDocument/272230, RO, consolidated version 08.08.2026]
- CNAIR (the national road company, official site cnadnr.ro, not cnair.ro) announced: "Începând cu 1 octombrie 2026, rovinieta, tichetul de rută și tariful TollRo se vor putea achita doar prin noul Sistem de Tarifare Rutieră Electronică". Sales in the current system (SIEGMCR) stop on 30 Sep 2026 at 23:59. From 1 Oct 2026 at 00:00, all new rovinietas are issued only through the TollRo platform. [https://www.cnadnr.ro/en/node/15933, RO, 25 Sep 2026]
- Official seller up to 30 Sep 2026: erovinieta.ro, the CNAIR portal (page footer "© C.N.A.I.R."). [https://www.erovinieta.ro/vignettes-portal-web/, RO, no date]
- Official seller from 1 Oct 2026: the TollRo portal (portal.etoll.ro / etoll.ro), operated by C.N.A.I.R. S.A., in Romanian and "English (United Kingdom) [beta]". [https://portal.etoll.ro/ and https://www.etoll.ro/, RO/EN, no date]
- Sales channels from 1 Oct 2026: the TollRo portal, the mobile app (iOS/Android), authorised distributors, CNAIR sales points, border crossing points, SMS and selected cash-payment locations. [https://www.cnadnr.ro/en/node/15933, RO, 25 Sep 2026]
- roviniete.ro is run by Scala Assistance SRL, an authorised distributor, not by CNAIR. [https://www.roviniete.ro/ro/info/intrebari-frecvente-tollro, RO, FAQ dated 31 Aug 2026]
- Rovinietas already bought stay valid: "Rovinietele pentru vehiculele de transport persoane (autoturisme, microbuze si autocare) deja cumpărate rămân valabile". [https://www.cnadnr.ro/en/node/15933, RO, 25 Sep 2026]
- Legea 226/2023 art. 24(2): from 1 Oct 2026, rovinietas issued under the old law stop being valid, except those for the vehicles in art. 4(1) (passenger vehicles and goods vehicles ≤ 3.5 t), which stay valid until they expire. Art. 24(4): CNAIR refunds unused rovinietas on written request. [https://legislatie.just.ro/public/DetaliiDocument/272230, RO, consolidated 08.08.2026]
- A bill registered in the Senate on 21 Sep 2026 would postpone the deadlines in Legea 226/2023 to 1 Apr 2027. It had not been adopted at the time of research, and CNAIR confirmed the 1 Oct 2026 start on 25 Sep. [https://www.cotidianul.ro/rovinieta-si-tollro-ar-putea-fi-amanate-din-nou-proiect-la-senat-pentru-mutarea-termenului/, RO, news, 23 Sep 2026]

### Rovinietă: who, where, how long

- Rovinieta applies to vehicles for passenger transport and to goods vehicles with a maximum authorised mass (MTMA) ≤ 3.5 t (art. 4(1)). TollRo, which charges by distance, applies only to goods vehicles > 3.5 t MTMA. Passenger cars stay on the time-based rovinieta. [https://legislatie.just.ro/public/DetaliiDocument/272230, RO, consolidated 08.08.2026] [https://www.cnadnr.ro/en/node/15933, RO, 25 Sep 2026]
- Where it applies (from 1 Oct 2026), art. 5(1): "rețeaua de drumuri naționale din România, cu excepția sectoarelor de drum național aflate în intravilanul municipiilor". [https://legislatie.just.ro/public/DetaliiDocument/272230, RO, consolidated 08.08.2026]
- Durations (art. 5(2)): "o zi, 10 zile, 30 de zile, 60 de zile și 12 luni". There is no 7-day and no 90-day rovinieta in 2026. [https://legislatie.just.ro/public/DetaliiDocument/272230, RO, consolidated 08.08.2026] [https://www.cnadnr.ro/sites/default/files/pagini-statice/Pret%20ROVINIETE%20Septembrie%202026.pdf, RO, Sep 2026]
- Validity, art. 6(4)–(5): it "începe de la ora 0.00 a primei zile și expiră la ora 24.00 a ultimei zile". If bought for the same day, validity "începe la ora înregistrării informațiilor", i.e. from the moment of purchase, not retroactively. [https://legislatie.just.ro/public/DetaliiDocument/272230, RO, consolidated 08.08.2026]
- Responsibility (art. 6): "Responsabilitatea deținerii rovinietei valabile revine în exclusivitate utilizatorilor". The law makes no exception for foreign-registered vehicles. [https://legislatie.just.ro/public/DetaliiDocument/272230, RO, consolidated 08.08.2026]
- New tariff (from 1 Oct 2026): Ordinul MTI nr. 888/2026 of 22 Sep 2026, published in Monitorul Oficial nr. 818 of 25 Sep 2026. Prices are set directly in lei, VAT included, by vehicle category, Euro emission class and duration. [https://lege5.ro/Gratuit/ge4tqnzthazti/ordinul-nr-888-2026-pentru-aprobarea-categoriilor-de-vehicule-si-a-nivelului-rovinietei, RO, 25 Sep 2026]
- Order 888/2026 categories: A = "Vehicul de transport persoane cu maximum 9 locuri pe scaune (inclusiv conducătorul auto)"; B = goods vehicles ≤ 3.5 t; C = 10–23 seats; D = 24+ seats. The order has no motorcycle category. [lege5.ro, RO, 25 Sep 2026]
- Euro class in Order 888/2026: a vehicle without Euro class data is classed as Euro 0 (the most expensive band). Electric vehicles get the electric rate based on the energy-source entry (field P.3) in the registration certificate. [lege5.ro, RO, 25 Sep 2026] [https://www.untrr.ro/ro/1-octombrie-2026-se-modifica-tarifele-de-rovinieta.html, RO, 28 Sep 2026]
- The lei amounts in Order 888/2026 are updated every year by the consumer price index (art. 2: Tapl = Tact × IPC/100). 2027 prices will therefore differ. [lege5.ro, RO, 25 Sep 2026]
- Up to 30 Sep 2026, the tariff is set in EUR and converted to lei at a fixed rate for each month. For September 2026 the rate is 5.2584 lei per euro. [https://www.cnadnr.ro/sites/default/files/pagini-statice/Pret%20ROVINIETE%20Septembrie%202026.pdf, RO, Sep 2026]
- Increase of 1 Sep 2025 (Legea 141/2025), category A: 1 day 2.5 → 3.5 EUR, 10 days 3.3 → 6 EUR, 30 days 5.3 → 9.5 EUR, 60 days 8.4 → 15 EUR, 12 months 28 → 50 EUR. Rovinietas paid before 1 Sep 2025 stayed valid for their whole period. [https://cnadnr.ro/en/node/14591, RO, 29 Aug 2025]

### Rovinietă: enforcement and fines

- Checks use "terminale de interogare" and "sisteme de camere video pe rețeaua de drumuri naționale din România" (art. 18(4)), operated by CNAIR and the police, including automatically (art. 19(1)). [https://legislatie.just.ro/public/DetaliiDocument/272230, RO, consolidated 08.08.2026]
- Fine up to 30 Sep 2026 (OG 15/2002 as amended by Legea 141/2025, from 1 Sep 2025), passenger cars: 500–1,000 lei. Before that it was 275–550 lei. [https://cnadnr.ro/en/node/14591, RO, 29 Aug 2025]
- Fine from 1 Oct 2026 (Legea 226/2023 art. 18(1)–(3)): driving without a valid rovinieta is a "contravenție continuă", fined between "dublul rovinietei pentru 12 luni" and "de patru ori rovinieta pentru 12 luni". The amount is updated every year by CPI. [https://legislatie.just.ro/public/DetaliiDocument/272230, RO, consolidated 08.08.2026]
- Calculated from art. 18(2) and the 12-month values in Order 888/2026 for category A (my arithmetic, not an official table): electric 456–912 lei, Euro VI 508–1,016 lei, Euro IV–V 584–1,168 lei, Euro 0–III 660–1,320 lei. See Unverified.
- Time limit (art. 18(12)): the fine must be applied within 4 months of the offence. [https://legislatie.just.ro/public/DetaliiDocument/272230, RO, consolidated 08.08.2026]

### Other tolls on the way

- Fetești–Cernavodă bridges (A2, relevant from Constanța), up to 30 Sep 2026: from 8 Sep 2025, cars and vehicles ≤ 3.5 t pay 19.00 lei for one crossing or 38.00 lei for two, VAT included, valid 12 months from payment. Legal basis: annex 7 to OG 15/2002. [https://www.untrr.ro/ro/peaj-fete-ti-cernavoda-noi-tarife-din-8-septembrie-2025.html, RO, hauliers' association, 2025]
- Fetești–Cernavodă and Giurgeni–Vadu Oii from 1 Oct 2026: interim transport minister Radu Miruță announced "De la 1 octombrie dispare taxa de pod peste Dunăre. Și la Fetești, și la Vadu Oii." The rovinieta is still required on A2. [https://www.jurnaldeconstanta.ro/eliminarea-taxei-de-pod-de-la-fetesti-si-vadu-oii-incepand-cu-1-octombrie-comunicatul-ministrului-transporturilor/, RO, news, 23 Sep 2026] [https://www.promotor.ro/stiri-auto/veste-pentru-soferi-de-la-1-octombrie-dispar-taxele-de-pod-de-la-fetesti-si-vadu-oii-19566097, RO, news, 23 Sep 2026] (No CNAIR or MT primary text found; see Unverified.)
- Under Legea 226/2023 art. 3(2), an extra "tarif de trecere" may apply "numai pentru utilizarea podurilor, tunelurilor și trecătorilor de munte", i.e. not for motorway sections as such. [https://legislatie.just.ro/public/DetaliiDocument/272230, RO, consolidated 08.08.2026]

### Winter equipment (situational rule)

- OUG 195/2002 art. 102(1) pct. 28 makes it an offence, class IV fine: "conducerea autovehiculului pe drumurile publice acoperite cu zăpadă, gheață sau polei, fără ca acesta să fie dotat cu anvelope de iarnă". [https://soferonline.ro/codul-rutier/oug-195-2002/articolul-102, RO, mirror of the consolidated code, 2026] Official text: [https://legislatie.just.ro/public/detaliidocument/74028, RO, consolidated 25.08.2026]
- The police also keep the registration certificate, art. 112(1) lit. h^1: "autovehiculul nu este dotat, la circulația pe drumurile publice acoperite cu zăpadă, gheață sau polei, cu anvelope de iarnă". [https://soferonline.ro/codul-rutier/oug-195-2002/articolul-112, RO, mirror, 2026] [https://www.mai.gov.ro/precizari-privind-anvelopele-de-iarna/, RO, no date]
- Goods vehicles > 3.5 t and buses > 9 seats need winter tyres on the drive axle(s) or approved snow chains on those wheels. [https://www.mai.gov.ro/precizari-privind-anvelopele-de-iarna/, RO, no date]
- What counts as a winter tyre in Romania: tyres marked "M+S", "M.S.", "MS" or "M&S". Tyres marked only "all seasons" do not count. In Romania M+S is enough, but Germany (and Austria) require the 3PMSF alpine symbol, so the same tyres can be legal in RO and illegal in DE. [https://www.mai.gov.ro/precizari-privind-anvelopele-de-iarna/, RO, no date]
- There is no fixed calendar date; the obligation depends on road conditions. [https://www.mai.gov.ro/precizari-privind-anvelopele-de-iarna/, RO, no date] [https://www.avocatnet.ro/articol_62971/RAR-Legisla%C8%9Bia-nu-prevede-o-perioad%C4%83-exact%C4%83-in-care-s%C4%83-fie-utilizate-anvelopele-de-iarn%C4%83.html, RO, 2025]
- Fine amount: one punct-amendă is "5% din salariul de bază minim brut pe țară garantat" (OUG 195/2002 art. 98) [https://soferonline.ro/codul-rutier/oug-195-2002/articolul-98, RO, mirror, 2026]. The minimum gross wage is 4,325 lei from 1 Jul 2026 (HG 146/2026) [https://mmuncii.gov.ro/salariul-de-baza-minim-brut-pe-tara-garantat-in-plata-se-majoreaza/, RO, 2026]. So 1 point = 216.25 lei, and class IV (9–20 points) = 1,946.25–4,325 lei, a figure the Romanian Police confirmed. [https://www.digi24.ro/stiri/actualitate/social/cresc-amenzile-rutiere-de-la-1-iulie-2026-politia-romana-a-confirmat-cat-vor-plati-soferii-dupa-majorarea-salariului-minim-3838887, RO, news, 30 Jun 2026]
- Before 1 Jul 2026, 1 point = 202.5 lei, so class IV = 1,822.50–4,050 lei. [same Digi24 article, 30 Jun 2026]
- Scope, OUG 195/2002 art. 1: "Circulația pe drumurile publice a vehiculelor ... sunt supuse dispozițiilor prevăzute în prezenta ordonanță de urgență". The code has no exemption for foreign-registered vehicles. [https://legislatie.just.ro/public/detaliidocument/74028, RO, consolidated 25.08.2026]

### Mandatory equipment and borders

- OUG 195/2002 art. 8: "Autovehiculele, cu excepția celor din categoriile AM, A1, A2 și A, tractoarele agricole sau forestiere și tramvaiele trebuie să fie dotate cu trusă medicală de prim ajutor, două triunghiuri reflectorizante și un stingător de incendiu, omologate." Motorcycles are exempt. [https://legislatie.just.ro/public/detaliidocument/74028, RO, consolidated 25.08.2026] [https://soferonline.ro/codul-rutier/oug-195-2002/articolul-8, RO, mirror, 2026]
- Hi-vis vest: RAR (Registrul Auto Român) said a vest is mandatory in Romania only for vehicles > 3.5 t. [https://www.rarom.ro/?p=214, RO, 17 Jan 2007, old page; see Unverified]
- Romania has been a full Schengen member for land borders (Hungary, Bulgaria) since 1 Jan 2025, so there are no systematic checks at Nădlac II or Borș. [https://www.mediafax.ro/social/romania-complet-in-spatiul-schengen-fara-controale-la-granita-cu-bulgaria-si-ungaria-22629954, RO, news, 1 Jan 2025]
- The European Commission's current list of temporary internal border controls has no notification from Romania or Hungary. On the way west, Austria (land borders with Hungary, Slovenia, Czechia, Slovakia) and Germany (land borders including Austria) have controls from 16/09/2026 to 15/03/2027, so carry an ID card or passport. [https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/schengen/schengen-area/temporary-reintroduction-border-control_en, EN, list as of Sep 2026]

## Unverified

- Motorcycles: widely reported as not needing a rovinieta. Order 888/2026 has no motorcycle category (A–D only), but no CNAIR or law text found that states it explicitly. Legea 226/2023 art. 2 defines "vehicul" as a registered motor vehicle "având cel puțin două axe" or a combination, and whether that wording excludes motorcycles is a legal interpretation, not confirmed.
- Car + trailer: whether a caravan or light trailer towed by a category A car needs its own rovinieta or changes the category from 1 Oct 2026. Not found in Order 888/2026 or on CNAIR pages.
- Camper / motorhome ≤ 3.5 t: whether it falls under category A (passenger transport ≤ 9 seats) or B from 1 Oct 2026. Not found.
- Fine from 1 Oct 2026: whether the "rovinieta pentru 12 luni" in art. 18(2) means the rovinieta for the vehicle's own Euro class, so that the 456–1,320 lei range above holds. No CNAIR explanation found.
- Fetești–Cernavodă abolition from 1 Oct 2026: only the minister's statement reported by news sites. No CNAIR or mt.ro primary text found.
- Whether any toll other than the rovinieta applies to cars on A1 (Nădlac–Arad–Deva–Sibiu) in 2026. No official announcement found. Legea 226/2023 art. 3(2) limits extra tolls to bridges, tunnels and mountain passes.
