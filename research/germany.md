# Germany: what a private driver needs (car ≤ 3.5 t, camper, trailer, motorcycle)

Research date: 2026-09-29. Scope: a Romanian-registered private vehicle driving to or through Germany (for example via Passau, Suben or Walserberg from Austria).

## Facts

### Tolls

- Germany has no motorway toll or vignette for passenger cars. The federal toll (BFStrMG § 1) applies only to motor vehicles and combinations "die für den Güterkraftverkehr bestimmt sind oder verwendet werden" with a "technisch zulässige Gesamtmasse mehr als 3,5 Tonnen", on federal motorways and federal roads. [https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bfstrmg/__1.html, DE, current consolidated text]
- The Federal Transport Ministry confirms the toll threshold of > 3.5 t for goods vehicles since 1 Jul 2024, charged on all federal roads (about 40,000 km of Bundesstraßen) since 1 Jul 2018. [https://www.bmv.de/SharedDocs/DE/Artikel/StV/Strassenverkehr/lkw-maut.html, DE, 27 Feb 2026]
- Craftsmen's exemption: craftsmen's goods vehicles under 7.5 t are exempt under conditions (BFStrMG § 1(2)). This does not matter for private cars. [https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bfstrmg/__1.html, DE, current]
- The car toll failed in court: on 18 Jun 2019 the Court of Justice of the EU ruled in case C-591/17 (Austria v Germany) that the German infrastructure charge for passenger cars, combined with a vehicle-tax refund for German keepers, discriminated against drivers from other member states and was contrary to EU law (press release 75/19). [https://curia.europa.eu/juris/liste.jsf?num=C-591/17, EN, 18 Jun 2019]
- Current plans: in June 2026, Federal Transport Minister Patrick Schnieder (CDU) had a spokesperson reply to a construction-industry proposal: "Die Einführung einer Maut für Pkw ist nicht vorgesehen und wird derzeit nicht verfolgt." The CDU/CSU–SPD coalition agreement does not mention a car toll. [https://www.nordkurier.de/politik/kommt-jetzt-eine-maut-fuer-autofahrer-verkehrsminister-spricht-ein-machtwort-4658714, DE, news, 23 Jun 2026]
- Exceptions for cars: two privately financed tunnels charge a fee, the Herrentunnel in Lübeck ("Nutzungsgebühr für den Herrentunnel") and the Warnowtunnel in Rostock. Neither is on a Romania–southern Germany route. [https://www.herrentunnel.de/, DE, no date] (Prices: see Unverified.)
- Border checks: Germany carries out temporary internal border controls at all its land borders, including Austria, from 16/09/2026 to 15/03/2027. Austria does so at its borders with Hungary, Slovenia, Czechia and Slovakia over the same period. Carry an ID card or passport. [https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/policies/schengen/schengen-area/temporary-reintroduction-border-control_en, EN, list as of Sep 2026]

### Umweltzonen (low-emission zones) and the green sticker

- Status on 26 Aug 2026: 35 active Umweltzonen, 23 lifted. In München, Stuttgart, Berlin, Frankfurt am Main and Köln, stage 3 (green sticker only) is in force. [https://gis.uba.de/website/umweltzonen/, DE, content 26 Aug 2026 / technical 27 Aug 2026]
- Foreign vehicles need a sticker too. Munich: "Auch Gäste und Touristen benötigen eine Plakette" [https://www.muenchen.de/verkehr/autos/umweltzone-und-diesel-fahrverbot-muenchen-alle-infos, DE, no date]. Berlin: "Auch Besucher aus dem Ausland, die mit ihrem Fahrzeug nach Berlin kommen, brauchen für ihr Fahrzeug eine Umweltplakette." [https://www.berlin.de/sen/uvk/umwelt/luft/luftreinhaltung/umweltzone/fahrzeuge-aus-dem-ausland/, DE, no date]
- No sticker is needed to enter Germany itself, only to enter a signed Umweltzone: "bei Einfahrt in das Staatsgebiet Deutschlands keine Plakette erforderlich". The sticker "ist in allen Umweltzonen Deutschlands gültig". [https://www.bundesumweltministerium.de/themen/luft-laerm-mobilitaet/luft/umweltplakette-umweltzone, DE, 6 Jul 2022]
- Which cars get green: diesel "Euro 4 oder Euro 3 mit PMS und besser" (as a guide, first registered from 1 Jan 2006); petrol/gas "Euro 1 und besser" (from 1 Jan 1993); electric vehicles get green automatically. [https://www.bundesumweltministerium.de/themen/luft-laerm-mobilitaet/luft/umweltplakette-umweltzone, DE, 6 Jul 2022]
- Foreign vehicles are classed either by proof of the EU emission standard or, failing that, by the date of first registration (diesel from 1 Jan 2006 → green; petrol from 1 Jan 1993 → green). "Im Einzelfall sind Abweichungen möglich." The plate number must be written on the sticker for it to be valid. [https://www.berlin.de/sen/uvk/_assets/umwelt/luft/luftreinhaltung/umweltzone/umweltzone_info_ausl_touristen.pdf, DE, PDF on berlin.de, partly outdated (see Outdated claims)]
- Who issues stickers: registration authorities, technical inspection bodies (TÜV, DEKRA, GTÜ, etc.) and authorised workshops, for foreign vehicles too. The vehicle does not have to be shown; the registration document is enough. [berlin.de tourist PDF, as above] [https://www.bundesumweltministerium.de/themen/luft-laerm-mobilitaet/luft/umweltplakette-umweltzone, DE, 6 Jul 2022]
- Documents: "Zulassungsbescheinigung Teil I / Fahrzeugschein / eine Herstellerbescheinigung (in Kopie)" (Berlin LABO) [https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/121490/, DE, no date]. TÜV NORD asks for a copy of the vehicle licence as an upload of up to 4 MB [https://www.tuev-nord.de/en/services/driving-licence-and-vehicle/environmental-sticker/buy-an-environmental-badge/, EN, no date].
- Buying online from Romania:
  - TÜV NORD: "14.90 euros incl. VAT and dispatch by post against invoice. For vehicles not registered in Germany, we charge 19.90 euros." [tuev-nord.de, EN, no date]
  - DEKRA: "19,90 Euro inkl. deutscher MwSt. und Versand", paid by invoice; the page does not say whether it ships abroad. [https://www.dekra.de/de/feinstaubplakette-bestellen/, DE, no date]
  - Berlin registration authority (LABO): from "5,95 Euro (mindestens) je Plakette", online with BundID or an eID card, processing "3 bis 4 Wochen"; foreign applicants should apply "ca. 3 Wochen vor dem Berlin-Aufenthalt". [https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/121490/, DE, no date]
  - At an issuing office in person: 5–10 € per the federal environment ministry, plus postage if mailed. [bundesumweltministerium.de, DE, 6 Jul 2022]
- Fine for driving in an Umweltzone without a valid sticker (or with a banned diesel): 100 € (BKatV annex, no. 153: "Mit einem Kraftfahrzeug trotz Verkehrsverbotes zur Verminderung schädlicher Luftverunreinigungen (Zeichen 270.1, 270.2) am Verkehr teilgenommen"). [https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bkatv_2013/anlage.html, DE, current]
- The fine carries no points: BKat no. 153 is not listed in the points table (FeV Anlage 13) [https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/anlage_13.html, DE, current], and Stuttgart confirms "There is no entry of points in the driver's license register". [https://www.stuttgart.de/en/leben/mobilitaet/dieselverkehrsverbot/dieselverkehrsverbot, EN, as of 24 Feb 2026]
- With fees the total is 128.50 € (Munich and Stuttgart official pages). [https://stadt.muenchen.de/infos/umweltzone-muenchen.html, DE, 2026] [stuttgart.de, EN, 24 Feb 2026]
- Exempt from Umweltzone bans (35. BImSchV Anhang 3): two- and three-wheeled motor vehicles, so motorcycles need no sticker. Also exempt are historic vehicles with a German H or 07 plate (§ 9(1) / § 17 FZV), vehicles of severely disabled persons (aG, H, Bl) and others. [https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bimschv_35/anhang_3.html, DE, current]
- Zones lifted 2024–2026 (UBA): Freiburg 22 Apr 2025; Mainz 1 Oct 2025; Hannover 22 Feb 2024; Heidenheim, Heilbronn, Herrenberg and Leonberg/Hemmingen 1 Jan 2024; Reutlingen, Tübingen, Ulm and Neu-Ulm 4 Jun 2024; Mannheim and Mühlacker 1 Aug 2024. München, Stuttgart, Berlin, Frankfurt and Köln were not lifted and are still active. [https://gis.uba.de/website/umweltzonen/, DE, 26 Aug 2026]
- Hamburg has no Umweltzone: it is not in the UBA list of 35 active zones. [https://gis.uba.de/website/umweltzonen/, DE, 26 Aug 2026]

### Diesel driving bans

- Munich: the Umweltzone covers the area on and within the Mittlerer Ring, the ring road itself included since 1 Feb 2023. "Bis auf Weiteres gilt eine Zufahrtsbeschränkung für Diesel-Fahrzeuge mit den Abgasnormen Diesel, Euro 4/IV und schlechter in der Münchner Umweltzone (inklusive Mittlerer Ring)." The sign reads "Diesel (außer Lieferverkehr und Anwohner) erst ab Euro 5/V frei". [https://stadt.muenchen.de/infos/umweltzone-muenchen.html, DE, 2026]
- Munich exceptions: delivery traffic and residents (registered in the zone, second homes included) are exempt by signage, which does not help a visitor. Euro 4/5 diesels with NOx retrofits below 270 mg/km are exempt under § 47(4a) BImSchG. Euro 5 diesels with a green sticker may enter. [https://stadt.muenchen.de/infos/umweltzone-muenchen.html, DE, 2026] [https://www.muenchen.de/verkehr/autos/umweltzone-und-diesel-fahrverbot-muenchen-alle-infos, DE, no date]
- Munich, no Euro 5 ban in 2026: the city page lists only the Euro 4 restriction. The ninth update of the clean-air plan (in force since 20 Oct 2025) adds Tempo 30 on the Landshuter Allee (Arnulfstraße to Toni-Merkens-Weg) and adjusted traffic lights at Moosacher Straße, not a Euro 5 ban. The NO2 limit of 40 µg/m³ has been met citywide since 2024. [https://stadt.muenchen.de/infos/umweltzone-muenchen.html, DE, 2026]
- Stuttgart: the Umweltzone covers the whole city. "Since January 1, 2019, a year-round traffic ban has been in effect in the Stuttgart Low Emission Zone - i.e., in the entire city area - for vehicles with diesel engines in emission class Euro 4/IV and worse." [https://www.stuttgart.de/en/leben/mobilitaet/dieselverkehrsverbot/dieselverkehrsverbot, EN, as of 24 Feb 2026]
- Stuttgart, Euro 5: "Since July 1, 2020, a zonal traffic ban for diesel vehicles of emission class Euro 5/V and worse ... in the Stuttgart basin and in the districts of Bad Cannstatt, Feuerbach and Zuffenhausen - the so-called 'small environmental zone'." It "is subject to further court decisions" but is in force. Euro 6/VI diesels are not affected. Three access routes are exempt. [stuttgart.de, EN, 24 Feb 2026] UBA lists the Euro 5 zone as "außer Lieferverkehr und Anwohner". [https://gis.uba.de/website/umweltzonen/, DE, 26 Aug 2026]
- Hamburg: the diesel through-traffic bans on Max-Brauer-Allee and Stresemannstraße (in place since May 2018) were lifted on 13 Sep 2023, because the NO2 limits were permanently met. [https://www.hamburg.de/politik-und-verwaltung/behoerden/bukea/aktuelles/pressemeldungen/2023-09-13-bukea-luftreinhalteplan-235376, DE, 13 Sep 2023] [https://www.polizei.hamburg/ende-der-dieseldurchfahrtsverbote-787116, DE, 2023]
- Berlin: all street-specific diesel bans (Euro 5 and worse) were lifted by autumn 2022. Four of eight sections went in June 2021, and Leipziger Straße, Hermannstraße, Silbersteinstraße and Alt-Moabit followed in September 2022. The zone inside the S-Bahn ring (green since 1 Jan 2010) stays. [https://www.berlin.de/sen/uvk/presse/pressemitteilungen/2023/pressemitteilung.1295939.php, DE, 17 Feb 2023]
- Frankfurt am Main: there are no diesel bans beyond the green-sticker zone (active since 1 Oct 2008, green only since 1 Jan 2012). "Aktuell wird daher nicht mit weiteren Diesel-Fahrverboten gerechnet." [https://www.frankfurt-main.ihk.de/branchenthemen/verkehr-logistik/verkehrspolitik/strasse/fahrverbote-in-frankfurt-am-main-5247622, DE, chamber of commerce, no date] [https://gis.uba.de/website/umweltzonen/, DE, 26 Aug 2026]
- Köln: green only since 1 Jul 2014, in an expanded zone of about 137 km² since 1 Oct 2019. UBA lists no diesel through-traffic restriction. [https://www.stadt-koeln.de/artikel/03399/index.html, DE, map as of Sep 2019 / streets May 2020] [https://gis.uba.de/website/umweltzonen/, DE, 26 Aug 2026]
- Other active diesel restriction (UBA): Darmstadt, on sections of Heinrichstraße and Hügelstraße since 1 Jun 2019. [https://gis.uba.de/website/umweltzonen/, DE, 26 Aug 2026]

### Winter tyres

- § 2(3a) StVO: "Der Führer eines Kraftfahrzeuges darf dies bei Glatteis, Schneeglätte, Schneematsch, Eisglätte oder Reifglätte nur fahren, wenn alle Räder mit Reifen ausgerüstet sind, die ... den Anforderungen des § 36 Absatz 4 der Straßenverkehrs-Zulassungs-Ordnung genügen." The rule depends on road conditions, not on a calendar date. It applies to all drivers on German roads, foreign-registered cars included. [https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__2.html, DE, current]
- Motorcycles are exempt: § 2(3a) "gilt nicht für" (among others) "einspurige Kraftfahrzeuge". [https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvo_2013/__2.html, DE, current]
- § 36(4) StVZO defines a winter tyre by the alpine symbol: "mit dem Alpine-Symbol (Bergpiktogramm mit Schneeflocke) nach der Regelung Nr. 117 der Wirtschaftskommission der Vereinten Nationen für Europa (UNECE)". [https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stvzo_2012/__36.html, DE, current]
- The M+S transition has ended: "Alte 'M+S-Reifen' bis zum Herstellungsdatum 31. Dezember 2017 können noch bis 30. September 2024 verwendet werden". Since 1 Oct 2024, only tyres with the alpine (3PMSF) symbol count. [https://www.polizei.bayern.de/verkehr/verkehrsrecht/005372/index.html, DE, Bavarian police, 26 Aug 2026]
- Fines (BKatV annex): no. 5a, driving in those conditions without compliant tyres, 60 €; no. 5a.1, the same "mit Behinderung", 80 €; no. 213a, the vehicle keeper who allows it, 75 €. [https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bkatv_2013/anlage.html, DE, current]
- Points: FeV Anlage 13 section 3 ("mit einem Punkt") lists "die Straßenbenutzung durch Fahrzeuge – 4.1, 4.2, 5a, 5a.1, 6", so 1 point for no. 5a and 5a.1. The keeper offence 213a ("Bereifung und Laufflächen 212, 213, 213a") is also 1 point. [https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fev_2010/anlage_13.html, DE, current]

## Unverified

- Motorhomes > 3.5 t: BALM's criteria for toll exemption (fixed living equipment, private use), as reported by ADAC and others. BALM's own FAQ page as fetched did not mention motorhomes. BFStrMG only covers vehicles "für den Güterkraftverkehr bestimmt oder verwendet".
- Car + trailer ≥ 3.5 t combined: not tolled because it is not goods transport. This is an inference from BFStrMG § 1; no explicit official statement found.
- Herrentunnel and Warnowtunnel car prices for 2026. Not fetched: the Herrentunnel fee table was not on the fetched page, and warnowtunnel.de had a certificate error.
- TÜV SÜD price for foreign vehicles (17.50 €, up to 2 weeks delivery), from a search snippet only; tuvsud.com returned 403.
- Delivery time of mailed stickers to Romania: only Berlin's advice ("ca. 3 Wochen", or "mindestens zwei Wochen" in the older PDF) is official. There is no carrier-specific figure.
- Whether the Berlin LABO online application works for foreigners without BundID/eID.
- Whether a trailer or caravan needs its own sticker. Umweltzone bans apply to motor vehicles, so likely no, but no official statement was fetched.
- Munich city council decision of 26 Jul 2023 to suspend stage 2 (Euro 5) "bis auf weiteres" and drop stage 3; BayVGH ruling of 21 Mar 2024 and BVerwG decision of 22 Oct 2024. These come from secondary summaries; not fetched from muenchen.de or the courts.
- Stuttgart: news (Stuttgarter Nachrichten, TrueAuto, 2025–2026) about a possible relaxation of the Euro 5 ban and the state minister's refusal to lift it. Not confirmed on stuttgart.de, which still shows the ban as of 24 Feb 2026.
- BKatV increments for winter-tyre offences with danger (100 €) or accident (120 €), widely reported. The fetched BKatV annex only showed 5a (60 €) and 5a.1 (80 €).
- Whether the Infrastrukturabgabengesetz (InfrAG) was formally repealed (Bundestag Drucksache 20/8092, 23 Aug 2023, appears to be a repeal bill). The PDF could not be read.

## Update 1 Oct 2026

- New federal transport minister Steffen Bilger (CDU) said on a car toll: "kein Projekt, für das ich jetzt eine Initiative starten werde". [https://www.nordkurier.de/politik/von-der-pkw-maut-laesst-bilger-die-finger-weg-4798187, DE, press, 20 Aug 2026] § 1 BFStrMG still limits the toll to goods vehicles over 3.5 t.
- Munich (green sticker; diesel Euro 4 and older banned inside the Mittlerer Ring, Euro 5 allowed) and the 3PMSF winter-tyre rule rechecked: unchanged.
